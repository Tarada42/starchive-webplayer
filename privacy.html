Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

STArchive (the "app") is a music-listening app that lets you browse and play DJ sets from the ST Archive, see which DJs are live, and save favourites and listening history. This policy describes what data the app handles. It is intentionally minimal, because the app is minimal: we collect no personal information.

What we do NOT collect

STArchive does not collect or require any account, name, email address, phone number, or other personally identifiable information. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, phonebook, SMS, or device storage. It contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics beacons, and no cross-device tracking identifiers.

Data the app does handle

The app streams publicly available music and DJ information from the ST Archive servers over the internet. To function, the app uses:

  • A local anonymous session identifier. The app stores a random, generated token on your device to remember your favourites and listening history between uses. This token is not connected to your identity and cannot be used to identify you.
  • Playback and favourite/history data. Your favourites and history are stored so the app can show them back to you. This data is tied only to the anonymous session token and is not shared with third parties.
  • Device permissions. The app requests the Internet permission (to stream audio), a foreground media-playback permission (to keep playing while the screen is off), and notification permission (so you can opt in to receiving an alert when a favourite DJ goes live). None of these reveal personal data about you.

What we do with your data

We use the session data solely to provide the app's features (favourites, history, live-DJ alerts). We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. We do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Third-party content

The app plays streaming media and shows DJ thumbnails that are publicly served by the ST Archive and (for some avatars) Twitch. Accessing these may cause the relevant provider to observe standard requests (e.g. IP address), as it would for any visitor to a public website. STArchive does not control those providers' data practices. The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data on its behalf.

Data retention & deletion

Your favourites and history are stored alongside the anonymous session token. You can clear your history at any time using the app's "Clear history" control. Deleting the app (or clearing its data) removes the local token and its associated stored data.
